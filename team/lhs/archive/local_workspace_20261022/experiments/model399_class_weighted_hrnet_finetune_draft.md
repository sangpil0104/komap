## Why this is a distinct candidate

The active Model392 runner passes no `--ce-class-weights`; `ComboLoss` therefore uses unweighted CE + class-mean Dice, alongside the class-agnostic boundary-head loss. The trainer already exposes `--ce-class-weights` in `[Al, Al3Ni, Primary Si, Eutectic Si]` order. The loss module documents the reason for avoiding inverse-frequency weights: an earlier run showed roughly 1.35x predicted Eutectic-Si area with about 1px boundary bleed. Model399 would test whether the teammate's paired training-weight/prior-correction recipe avoids that failure, with explicit thin-class safety gates.

**Implementation feasibility checked 2026-10-04 (no training):** the local trainer SHA-256 `af143d67d86dcf6f718db2de2b373d67ba51eedb8e55dc0ae3da9a6f45fcc815` exactly matches the Model392 locked trainer source and the P100 copy. Its CLI supports `--init-checkpoint` (loads model state and starts a fresh optimizer), `--epochs`, `--lr`, `--ce-class-weights`, `--normalize-width`, and `--val-every 0`. The exact locked script's help was checked in `/workspace/venv-komap` with CUDA disabled. One preflight gap: the trainer checks an init checkpoint's `model_config`, but does not compare its saved `preprocessing_config` against the newly computed fold target width/scale. A Model399 runner must explicitly assert that match from the locked checkpoint and fold-train image metadata, and fail closed on mismatch. This confirms the proposed short continuation can use the existing trainer without an unreviewed model-code fork; it does not authorize training or validate the proposed estimand.

The teammate-shared HRNet-W48/U-Net++ report reached 81.55% image-mean valid mIoU after training with inverse-train-mask-frequency CE weights and dividing predicted probabilities by those weights. This is a reported result, not an independently reproduced result. The shared report also cites a five-fold ResNet50 correction gain of `+1.18 pp`, below the `+2.35 pp` valid gain; that is supporting but cross-architecture evidence, not a replication on HRNet. Prior local analogues are not the same experiment: Model22 combined weighted CE/Dice with changed safe augmentation and gained `+0.27 pp` over its earlier baseline, so the weight effect was not isolated; Model280/281 compared weighted/equal CE on an early-stopped 4x EfficientNet-B4 run and differed by only `+0.0591 pp` at epoch 4; Model330 applied inverse-frequency correction to an **unweighted** Model327 route and lost `4.3153 pp` on reused valid. Model376 used per-pixel CE weights based on GT-boundary distance, not per-class inverse frequency, and improved HRNet grouped OOF by `+0.2166 pp` with a CI spanning zero. These results argue against blindly applying a correction, but do not test weighted training and its matching correction together on HRNet.

The supplied `KoMaP_code.zip` is a separate B4 reference, not implementation evidence for that HRNet report: its `train.py` uses unweighted CE + Dice and defaults to area-proportional image sampling with ordinary random crops. It has no inverse-frequency CE-weight option or rare-centered crop sampler. Therefore the screenshot-reported weighted HRNet result remains a recipe description without matching source in that ZIP; its sampler may also differ from Model392's, which is why the current crop-sampling interaction must be resolved before a transfer experiment is locked.

## Train-only crop-prior audit and implications

Model392 uses width-normalized 512px patches, 12 crops/image/epoch, `positive_sample_prob=0.5`, with Al3Ni/Eutectic-Si anchors selected equally when both exist. A train-only audit used folds 1–4, Model392 coordinate-cap seeds 1 and 2, a 70-epoch native-crop Monte Carlo, then one probe epoch and a preregistered ten-epoch repeat through the exact resize and Albumentations pipeline. Only the paired outer-training images/masks were read; CUDA was disabled. The crop sampler used independent deterministic per-image Monte Carlo streams, so this estimates the locked crop rule rather than reproducing incidental DataLoader worker scheduling byte-for-byte. Neither augmentation probe is an exact measurement of the realized training DataLoader stream. See [audit code](model399_crop_prior_audit.py), [one-epoch results](results/model399_crop_prior_audit.json), and [ten-epoch results](results/model399_crop_prior_audit_aug10.json).

Averaged over the eight fold/seed cells, the original one-epoch post-augmentation crop prior `q` divided by pooled whole-mask prior `p` was `[1.0168, 0.9553, 0.9892, 1.0444]` in `[Al, Al3Ni, Primary Si, Eutectic Si]` order. Per-fold/seed ranges were Al `[1.0076,1.0284]`, Al3Ni `[0.9301,0.9768]`, Primary Si `[0.9502,1.0428]`, and Eutectic Si `[1.0076,1.0812]`. Under uniform crops, the corresponding mean `q/p` was `[1.0118,0.9550,1.0476,1.0081]`. The preregistered ten-epoch precision repeat, with exact training-mask hash matches, estimated rare-centered `q/p` as `[1.0135,0.9655,0.9836,1.0414]`; its fold/seed ranges were Al `[1.0067,1.0215]`, Al3Ni `[0.9534,0.9842]`, Primary Si `[0.9545,1.0121]`, and Eutectic Si `[1.0062,1.0646]`. Ten-epoch rare-centered minus uniform mean crop-pixel shares were `[+0.096,+0.358,−0.685,+0.231]` percentage points, with the same sign in all eight cells for Al3Ni, Primary Si, and Eutectic Si. Thus 50% anchor probability is not a 50% pixel-prior shift. Results also include mean-per-image source priors because the competition metric is image-wise; that statistic is not automatically the target pixel prior.

Using the mean-per-image source prior `p_imgmean`, ten-epoch rare-centered `q/p_imgmean` means were `[1.0026,1.0102,0.9585,0.9995]`, with fold/seed ranges Al `[1.0002,1.0071]`, Al3Ni `[1.0052,1.0179]`, Primary Si `[0.9334,0.9828]`, and Eutectic Si `[0.9942,1.0042]`. Uniform-crop means were `[1.0009,0.9959,1.0292,0.9677]`. Maximum cellwise absolute normalized-weight differences `|1/q−1/p_imgmean|` were `[0.0062,0.0196,0.0690,0.0443]`, somewhat smaller than against pooled `p` for Al3Ni and Eutectic Si. Since the score averages per-image IoUs, `p_imgmean` is a relevant descriptive comparator, but is not mathematically the target prior that maximizes image-wise IoU.

This weakens the argument that the current rare-centered sampler requires a large correction, but does not settle whether static inverse-prior CE helps: the four-class source imbalance itself remains substantial, Dice breaks the pure weighted-CE posterior argument, and our previous unweighted-only prior correction was harmful. In an ideal label-shift model, if training crop prior is `q`, weighted CE yields logits tilted by both `q` and `w`; mapping to target prior `p` would use `z−log(w)−log(q/p)`. The literal teammate-style `z−log(w)` implicitly assumes `q=p`. These alternatives must not be silently conflated, and all calculations remain empirical for CE+Dice+boundary training.

As a scale check, normalizing each fold-local inverse-prior vector to arithmetic mean 1 and averaging folds/seeds yields `[0.229,0.499,1.391,1.881]` for pooled-source `1/p`, `[0.232,0.534,1.387,1.847]` for inverse mean-per-image source prior, and `[0.2280,0.5218,1.4271,1.8231]` for the ten-epoch sampler-aware `1/q` (class order `[Al, Al3Ni, Primary Si, Eutectic Si]`). `1/q` differs only moderately from `1/p` on average, but either scheme gives Eutectic Si roughly eight times Al's relative CE weight. Maximum fold/seed absolute ten-epoch `1/q−1/p` weight differences are `[0.0035,0.0340,0.0728,0.0965]`. These are descriptive averages only; any eventual training must recompute exact weights from each outer-fold training masks and safety-gate Eutectic false positives.

Across the eight fold/seed cells, ten-epoch normalized `1/q` weight ranges were Al `[0.2234,0.2312]`, Al3Ni `[0.4881,0.5506]`, Primary Si `[1.3841,1.4660]`, and Eutectic Si `[1.8078,1.8346]`. Relative to the one-epoch probe, mean rare-centered crop shares changed by `[-0.1902,+0.2686,−0.0577,−0.0206]` points and the maximum per-cell normalized-weight changes were `[0.0063,0.0180,0.0461,0.0490]`. The ten-epoch estimate is less sensitive to a single augmentation-probe epoch, but `q` remains close to the source prior, so sampler-aware complexity may not be worth adding. This is still not evidence that any weighting/correction improves mIoU. See [ten-epoch result JSON](results/model399_crop_prior_audit_aug10.json), SHA-256 `d553338b83a53f6364233331212ecde090f13366e605370d21e786e7d2cfd19d`.

One distinct alternative for Claude to assess is semantic-segmentation Recall Loss. The primary paper studies Synthia and Cityscapes with DeepLab/ResNet and reports competitive mean IoU, but not KoMaP's metric. The pinned [metric implementation](https://github.com/PotatoTian/recall-semseg/blob/a3f7fc3d12468ac41e78740122d8820c087c7aa6/ptsemseg/metrics.py#L18-L36) accumulates a dataset-level confusion matrix then uses `nanmean` over class IoUs, so its reported metric is pooled/global mIoU with empty classes omitted—not image-wise four-class mIoU with empty-union=0. A pinned audit of the [RecallCrossEntropy source](https://github.com/PotatoTian/recall-semseg/blob/a3f7fc3d12468ac41e78740122d8820c087c7aa6/ptsemseg/loss/recall_loss.py#L10-L39) found it computes hard-argmax false-negative rates independently per mini-batch, then applies them to pixelwise CE. Thus a KoMaP adaptation would react to each batch of four patches rather than a stable epoch-level recall; gradient accumulation does not pool these statistics. The released code also assumes CUDA and its ignore-index remapping is brittle if a batch has fewer than two distinct false-negative classes. Separately, the pinned [reference trainer](https://github.com/PotatoTian/recall-semseg/blob/a3f7fc3d12468ac41e78740122d8820c087c7aa6/train.py#L828-L865) computes static `WeightedCE`/`BalancedCE` class counts from `valloader`, while the [Synthia config](https://github.com/PotatoTian/recall-semseg/blob/a3f7fc3d12468ac41e78740122d8820c087c7aa6/configs/deeplab_synthia.yml#L5-L20) identifies `train` and `val` as distinct splits. This leakage warning applies to those static-weight branches, not automatically to the main RecallCE run configured in the YAML. The static computation must not be reproduced: any adaptation must be device-safe, robust to empty/single-class errors, and estimate priors from outer-training masks only. The method's false-positive motivation is relevant to Step4, but no KoMaP evidence establishes its benefit. If Claude prefers this direction, replace the weighted-CE draft with a fresh paired-control protocol rather than adding a sweep.

A separate fixed train-OOF error-type audit for Model397 shows why the next intervention may need to be conditional rather than globally class-weighted. On all OOF images, Eutectic-Si present-image macro recall/precision was `72.36/75.28%` for `H` and `72.96/76.18%` for `C`; the predicted/GT area ratio was near `1.0` (`1.017/1.023`). On Step4, all 27 held-out images contain Eutectic Si, yet recall/precision was `64.73/73.87%` for H and `65.78/74.74%` for C, with predicted/GT area only `0.894/0.899`. Step4 still has both false negatives and false positives; the C arm has `553,902` Eutectic FN pixels and `379,332` FP pixels. Foldwise area ratios differ in direction, so a single global class weight may over-correct some specimens. The fixed blend's main Step4 Eutectic FN destination was Al3Ni (`311,765` H-arm pixels). These are selection-conditional training-OOF diagnostics, not independent performance evidence. Claude should assess whether this supports (A) global static weights, (B) adaptive Recall Loss, or instead a narrowly predeclared Step4-conditioned Eutectic expert/head; the latter would supersede this global class-weight draft and needs its own paired group-OOF protocol. [error audit](model397_train_oof_error_type_audit.py), [results](results/model397_train_oof_error_type_audit.json).

## Cost, limitations, and required review

At the observed Model392 rate (~138 s/epoch and ~156 minibatches/epoch), the paired four-fold/four-epoch seed-1 screen is roughly 1.2 hours of training plus setup and OOF inference; seed-2 confirmation roughly doubles that. Actual fold cost may differ. This is a short continuation probe, not a reproduction of the teammate's full-from-ImageNet recipe. Dice plus weighted CE means `z−log(w)` is an empirical transfer of the teammate's correction, not an exact posterior-calibration identity. Class weighting may worsen Eutectic Si or distort class boundaries; the gates are intended to catch that.

The review must choose **one** route and one correction before lock, not recommend a sweep. If it chooses Recall Loss or a conditioned expert, supersede this global-weight draft with a fresh paired-control protocol.
